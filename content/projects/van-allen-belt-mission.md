### The challenge

The International Space Challenge is an annual technical design competition organised by Singapore Space and Technology Ltd (SSTL) and Space Faculty. In the 2022 edition (September 2021 to February 2022), our team designed a space mission to collect data on the dynamics of the Van Allen Belts.

### My role

I led the mission and orbit design and analysis, designed the deorbiting mechanism and the electrical subsystems, and defined the work breakdown structure and the workflow. I knew little about the field at the time and my teammates were even newer, so I also taught and supported them and planned the work.

Distinction Award.
