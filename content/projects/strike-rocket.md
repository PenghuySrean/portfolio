### Recovery system

At the Dynamics and Control Laboratory of the Institute of Technology of Cambodia, I designed the recovery system of a student sounding rocket. I sized the parachute from drop tests that measured its drag coefficient, and I tested the ejection system.

### Static-fire test stand

I built the electronics and wrote the code for the first version of the static-fire test stand used to characterise the rocket motor, and I supported the MAIT and flight test campaigns.
