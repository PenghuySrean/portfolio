The Airbus Sloshing Rocket Workshop 2024 took place in Portugal, with five teams in the finals. I led the TU Berlin team Skyhawk Squadron from February to August 2024: I coordinated the project and its documentation, designed the flight computer needed to operate the rocket and acquire flight data, and developed the flight simulation. We placed 3rd.
