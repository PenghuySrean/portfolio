In Khmer, there is a dish called *samlor korko*, a soup of mixed vegetables. No single ingredient makes it good. It needs every flavour, even the bitter ones.

Life is the same. When it gets hard, we believe it is bad for us. But the more experience I gather, the more I realise those hard times were not really bad. I have grown faster through the difficult seasons than through the easy ones.

So if you are going through a hard time, try to understand it and accept it as it is. When you crawl back out of it, you will be a stronger and better person.

The bad is exactly what the good is made from.
