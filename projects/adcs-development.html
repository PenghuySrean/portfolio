1 · 6-DOF attitude–orbit simulator

Student-led satellite development project · Technische Universität Berlin · Apr 2024 – Aug 2024

Extended my earlier orbital simulator to six degrees of freedom. Modelled attitude sensors and actuators, simulated attitude determination (TRIAD) and B-dot detumbling, sized the actuators needed to detumble the satellite against disturbances, and estimated their power consumption as part of a preliminary ADCS design.

2 · ADCS demonstrator

Internship · Aug 2024 – Dec 2024

Worked on 1D and 3D spacecraft attitude control demonstrators: assembled the mechanical structure, set up the electronics and communication with ROS 2, wrote firmware for sensor reading and motor control, calibrated and fused the attitude sensors, and tested the actuators and attitude control.

What it adds up to

Design → simulation → algorithms → hardware testing

Two separate projects that together covered the ADCS development chain: from sizing and simulating the system and its algorithms to testing attitude determination and control on hardware.
