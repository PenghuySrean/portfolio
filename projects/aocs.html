Internship + student project · 2020 – 2024

AOCS: from simulation to hardware

Three projects that took me through attitude and orbit control from mission analysis to hardware testing: an orbital simulator for CubeSat lifetime prediction, a six-degree-of-freedom attitude–orbit simulator for actuator sizing and attitude determination and control algorithms, and 1D and 3D attitude control demonstrators.

Tools
ROS 2, MATLAB/Simulink, Python
Skills
Orbital simulation, Lifetime prediction, 6-DOF attitude–orbit simulation, Attitude determination and control, Sensor calibration and fusion, Hardware integration and testing

1 · LEO orbital simulator

APSARA-1 CubeSat project · Internship · Phnom Penh · Dec 2020 – Jun 2021

Developed an in-house orbital simulator for CubeSat lifetime prediction under nominal and off-nominal solar flux, as part of defining the mission concept and subsystems of APSARA-1, Cambodia’s first CubeSat project, developed in collaboration with the University of Tokyo. The work was published as a first-author paper.

2 · 6-DOF attitude–orbit simulator

Student-led satellite development project · Technische Universität Berlin · Apr 2024 – Aug 2024

Extended the simulator to six degrees of freedom to size the actuators needed to detumble the satellite against disturbances, modelled attitude sensors and actuators, and simulated attitude determination (TRIAD) and B-dot detumbling as part of a preliminary ADCS design.

3 · ADCS demonstrator

Internship · Aug 2024 – Dec 2024

Worked on 1D and 3D spacecraft attitude control demonstrators: assembled the mechanical structure, set up the electronics and communication with ROS 2, wrote firmware for sensor reading and motor control, and worked on sensor calibration and fusion, control simulation, deployment and testing.

What it adds up to

Mission analysis → modelling and simulation → algorithms → hardware

Although these were separate projects, together they covered the AOCS development chain: from analysing a mission and modelling its dynamics, to designing algorithms and testing them on hardware.
