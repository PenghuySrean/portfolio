My goal is to research and develop space missions and technology that lower the barrier to space access.

About

I'm a space engineer based in Berlin. I finished my MSc in Space Engineering at TU Berlin in August 2026, after an engineering degree in Mechanical Engineering at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia.

My work covers orbit and attitude simulation, trajectory optimisation, collision-risk software, satellite ADCS and embedded firmware, from CubeSat and rover projects to an ESA-funded space-safety suite.

I'm a National Point of Contact at the Space Generation Advisory Council, and I'm building Astrognosis to make space science accessible to students.

Current focus

I am interested in applying my knowledge in astrodynamics, attitude dynamics, control and estimation to design, operate and analyse missions, and to design AOCS subsystems for satellites in very low Earth orbit (VLEO), where they experience high drag disturbance and torque. I am also interested in studying propulsion systems for such missions. Open to space-engineering roles and PhD positions.
