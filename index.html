My goal is to do research and development of space missions and technology that lower the barriers to space.

About

I'm a space engineer by training. I finished my MSc in Space Engineering at Technische Universität Berlin in August 2026.

My work covers modelling and simulation of orbit and attitude dynamics and control, trajectory and mission design and analysis, subsystem design such as ADCS, communication, command and data handling and power, and space debris study.

My path into space began at my university in Cambodia, when I was a mechanical engineering student and a team of PhD students from the University of Tokyo ran a four-day space engineering workshop there. I had never imagined a career in space was possible from where I stood. That is why I work to lower the barriers to space, so that others in Cambodia who dream like I did can reach it too.

Current focus

I am interested in applying my knowledge in astrodynamics, attitude dynamics, control and estimation to design, operate and analyse missions, and to design AOCS subsystems for satellites in very low Earth orbit (VLEO), where they experience high drag disturbance and torque. I am also interested in studying propulsion systems for such missions. Open to space-engineering roles and PhD positions.
